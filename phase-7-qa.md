# Phase 7 manual QA

- [ ] Chrome: allow microphone access, connect headphones, record 30 seconds over an instrumental, stop, review, save, reload, and play the take.
- [ ] Confirm record starts playback at the current instrumental seek position and stops both sources on Stop or when the beat ends.
- [ ] Record without an instrumental; confirm plain vocal recording still works.
- [ ] Verify the mic indicator disappears after Stop, Discard, navigation, and unmount.
- [ ] Deny microphone permission, disconnect the microphone, and test a browser without MediaRecorder; confirm distinct guidance.
- [ ] Disconnect the network during take upload; confirm the take is saved to local pending storage and Retry succeeds after reconnecting.
- [ ] Rename and delete a take; verify delete confirmation and disappearance from the newest-first list.
- [ ] Play a take while instrumental playback is running, then start instrumental playback; confirm only one source plays.
- [ ] Hide the tab or lock the phone during recording, return, stop and save the take.
- [ ] Firefox, iOS Safari and Android Chrome: check permission prompts, codec selection, headphones notice and background behavior.
